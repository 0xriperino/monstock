# StockBack

### Memes meet markets. Every trade gives back.

[Explore StockBack](https://monstock-zeta.vercel.app)

StockBack is a memecoin launchpad built around a simple idea: **the activity a community creates should give something back to that community.**

Users can launch a meme token, choose its quote asset, and participate in the fees generated by its market. Token holders receive a share of trading fees, while people who lock that market's quote asset in StockVault participate in a separate reward allocation.

The result is one connected experience for creating a token, discovering its market, trading, collecting rewards, and putting those rewards to use.

> StockBack runs on Monad Testnet. MockSPYx, PYDX, NVDAX, GLDX and ANTHROPIC are demo quote tokens. They have no real asset backing, redemption rights or affiliation with the companies or issuers their names may reference.

## Why StockBack exists

A meme can attract attention and bring people together. Turning that attention into an ongoing reason to participate is a different challenge.

Launching a token is only the beginning. A community also needs a market people can understand, a transparent explanation of where trading fees go, and ways to participate beyond repeatedly buying and selling.

StockBack brings those pieces together. It connects the cultural appeal of memecoins with a shared trading asset and an explicit fee-sharing model. The aim is to make the relationship between market activity and community participation easy to follow.

## The problem we address

StockBack focuses on three questions:

- **What happens after a token launches?** A launch should lead into a usable market and an ongoing community experience.
- **Who participates in the activity a market generates?** The destination of trading fees should be visible, with a defined share for token holders and committed participants.
- **What can users do with their rewards?** Receiving a reward should connect naturally to a next step, such as keeping it in a wallet or allocating it to a locked position.

Our answer is a launchpad where token creation, trading, holder rewards, and locked participation are parts of the same product.

## What you can do with StockBack

### Launch your own meme

Create a token with a name, a ticker, a quote asset and an initial amount of liquidity in that asset.

Each token launches with a fixed supply of **1,000,000 tokens** and receives its own market and StockVault. The initial token supply enters the market, and the initial liquidity stays locked.

This gives every StockBack launch a clear starting point: a defined supply, a dedicated trading pair, and a reward structure attached to that market.

### Discover and trade markets

Browse launched tokens, search for a community, inspect its liquidity, and open its trading view.

Each StockBack market pairs a meme with its chosen quote asset: **MockSPYx, PYDX, NVDAX, GLDX or ANTHROPIC**. Buy a meme with that asset or sell a meme back into it, with an estimated output and a minimum received amount shown before submitting a trade. An asset selector scopes market discovery, wallet balances and portfolio totals, so amounts in different currencies are never added together.

### Participate as a token holder

Holding a meme token makes a user eligible for that market's holder reward allocation.

Rewards are paid in each market's quote asset, giving holders a way to receive a share of that market's activity. Users can claim those rewards into their wallets and decide how to use them next.

### Lock quote assets in StockVault

Users who want to commit their chosen quote asset for a longer period can deposit it into a market's StockVault.

A deposit can come from previously claimed holder rewards or the same quote asset already in the user's wallet. Each deposit creates its own position with a fixed lock period and an unlock date.

This creates another form of participation alongside holding or trading a meme token.

## How every trade gives back

StockBack charges a **1% trading fee**, calculated on the quote-asset side of a trade.

That fee is divided into three allocations:

| Recipient | Share of the trading fee | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meme token holders | **40%** | Let eligible holders participate in their market's trading activity. |
| The market's StockVault | **10%** | Fund rewards for users who lock the market's quote asset in that Vault. |
| Protocol treasury | **50%** | Allocate a defined share to the protocol treasury. |

For example, a buy using **100 MockSPYx** pays **1 MockSPYx** in fees. Of that fee, **0.4 MockSPYx** is allocated to holders, **0.1 MockSPYx** to the associated StockVault, and **0.5 MockSPYx** to the treasury. The remaining **99 MockSPYx** is used for the swap.

These percentages describe shares of the fee, rather than shares of the full trade amount. When an allocation has no eligible holders or active Vault positions, that allocation goes to the treasury.

## Holder rewards: ownership participates in activity

Each market collects its holder allocation during a **five-minute reward epoch**.

Once an epoch is ready, anyone can submit its settlement. Settlement distributes the accumulated holder allocation according to eligible meme token balances at that moment. The market's own token inventory is excluded.

Settled rewards remain associated with the accounts that earned them when tokens are subsequently transferred. A holder can claim their available quote-asset rewards into their wallet.

This is a settlement-time ownership model. It does not measure how long a user held tokens during the epoch, and settlement requires a transaction rather than running automatically in the background.

## StockVault: a commitment of time

Every meme market has its own StockVault, funded by that market's Vault fee allocation.

Users choose one of three lock periods:

| Lock period | Reward weight |
| --- | --- |
| **30 days** | **1.00×** |
| **90 days** | **1.25×** |
| **180 days** | **1.60×** |

The weight determines a position's share of the rewards allocated to that Vault. A longer lock gives the deposited amount more weight in that distribution.

**These weights are allocation multipliers, not interest rates or guaranteed returns.** Rewards depend on the trading fees that the associated market generates and the reward weight participating in its Vault.

A position's principal stays locked for the full selected period. After its unlock date, the owner can withdraw the principal together with its accrued rewards. There is no early withdrawal option. An unlocked position continues to participate until it is withdrawn.

## One connected user journey

StockBack is designed to keep the experience understandable from the first launch to a locked position:

1. **Connect a wallet** to participate on Monad Testnet.
2. **Choose a quote asset** and obtain demo tokens through its one-claim-per-wallet faucet.
3. **Launch a meme** with its own selected-asset market, or discover an existing one.
4. **Buy or sell tokens** in the selected market.
5. **Settle a ready epoch** to allocate the accumulated fees.
6. **Claim holder rewards** into the wallet.
7. **Choose what comes next:** keep the claimed quote tokens or lock it in a StockVault.
8. **Follow positions and rewards** from the portfolio and Vault views.

The same asset connects liquidity, trading fees, holder rewards, and Vault deposits, giving users a consistent way to move through the product.

## Who StockBack is for

**Creators** who want to give a meme community a dedicated market and a defined fee-sharing model from launch.

**Traders** who want to discover meme markets, see their trading liquidity, and understand the fee structure before participating.

**Holders** who want a way to participate in market activity without needing to sell their meme tokens to receive a holder reward.

**Longer-term participants** who prefer to commit quote tokens for a chosen period and share in a market's Vault reward allocation.

These roles can overlap. A creator can also be a trader, a holder can deposit claimed rewards, and a Vault participant can hold positions across multiple markets.

## What makes StockBack different

- **A choice of quote assets.** Launch communities around different demo assets while keeping each pool, its fees and its Vault in the same currency.
- **Participation beyond the launch.** A token's market, holder allocation, and dedicated Vault are connected from the beginning.
- **A transparent fee split.** Users can see how activity is allocated between holders, Vault participants, and the treasury.
- **Rewards that remain useful within the product.** Claimed quote tokens can stay in a wallet or become a Vault deposit.
- **A clear time commitment.** Vault users choose a lock period and reward weight with an explicit unlock date.
- **Wallet-based ownership.** A wallet is the user's identity; balances and positions come from the blockchain.

## The quote asset collection

StockBack offers five demo quote assets:

| Asset | Product role |
| --- | --- |
| MockSPYx | The original stock-inspired quote asset. Existing markets remain available. |
| PYDX | An additional demo quote asset for new meme communities. |
| NVDAX | A NVIDIA-inspired demo asset. |
| GLDX | A gold-inspired demo asset. |
| ANTHROPIC | An Anthropic-inspired demo asset. |

These are independent test tokens with 18 decimals. Each wallet can claim **10,000 tokens once per asset**, so claiming NVDAX does not consume its GLDX or PYDX faucet allocation. Native test MON is separate and pays network gas.

When launching, the creator chooses the quote asset before supplying liquidity. A MEME/NVDAX market uses NVDAX for buys, sell proceeds, trading fees, holder rewards and its StockVault. A MEME/GLDX market follows the same model in GLDX. Choosing an asset does not convert existing balances or migrate an existing market.

The collection lets users explore different asset-inspired communities without brokerage accounts, stock custody or external asset prices. None of these tokens represents real securities, gold ownership, a company investment or a claim on an issuer. Their names describe the demo concept only.

## The idea behind the product

StockBack brings three elements into one experience:

**The identity of a meme community. The activity of its market. The choice of how to participate.**

A user can create, trade, hold, claim, or commit assets for a longer period. Each action has a clear place in the product, and the fee-sharing model connects market activity back to the people participating in it.

That is the purpose of StockBack: to explore how a meme launch can become an ongoing market experience with visible, understandable participation in its trading fees.
